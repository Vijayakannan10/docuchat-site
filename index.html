How it works

Three steps, no typing of long documents

1

Open a file

Choose a PDF, pick photos of pages, or scan a page with your camera.

2

Ask a question

Type it, or tap a suggestion like "Who wrote this?" or "Show emails".

3

Get the line

See the answer with its page number. Copy it, or reopen the chat later from Recent files.

Example

Like asking a person who has read the file

How many pages is this?
This PDF has 1 page.
Show phone numbers
Mobile: 9XXXXXXXXX (p. 1). Numbers are shown as they appear in your file.
Features

Built for everyday papers

Offline answers

Search and answers work without internet, using the text in your file.

Smart mode

Turn on AI answers for harder questions. Add your own free Gemini key.

Recent files

Reopen any file with its chat. Search, rename, pin to the top, or delete.

Dark mode

Easy on the eyes at night. Your choice is kept with your account.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your phone

  • Files, page text and chats are saved only on your phone.
  • Sign-in uses your Google account. We store only your name, phone number and email for your profile.
  • Smart mode sends your question and the document text to Google only when you turn it on.
